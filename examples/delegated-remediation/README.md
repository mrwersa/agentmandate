# Joint grant and quantity repair

The [delegation repair guide](../../docs/delegation-remediation.md) contains the
complete command and result interpretation.

Every review, actor and capture here is synthetic. The two-hop chain permits
read/write tools; it cannot authorize the manifest's irreversible purge tool.
The export quantity also exceeds its run budget. A joint repair removes purge
and lowers export's ceiling while preserving two required exports. Neither
grant evidence nor surviving declared effects change.

The source digest identifies this capture's bytes. The reader does not
authenticate an issuer or independently derive the reviewed deployment mapping
from the capture. This example supplies no operational approval.
